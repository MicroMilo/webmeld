# WebMeld brand

WebMeld's mark is built from two browser-like surfaces that meet around a white **W**. The split form represents the product's job: bringing a user's design intent into the webpage already in front of them.

[简体中文](BRAND.zh-CN.md)

| Asset | Use |

| Mist | `#F7F5FF` | Light brand background |

## Usage

- Prefer the full-color mark on white, mist, or another quiet light background.
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least one quarter of its width.
- Use the provided 16 px icon at very small sizes; do not scale down the 512 px asset in extension manifests.
- Do not recolor the two halves, stretch the mark, add shadows, or place content over the white seam.
- The product name is written **WebMeld**, with a capital **W** and **M**.

The editable SVG files are the source of truth. Raster exports are derivatives and may be regenerated when the mark changes.
